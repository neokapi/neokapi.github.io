Keep terms consistent

Goal: decide once how your key terms carry across languages — product names, UI actions, the words your domain depends on — and have kapi flag anything that drifts before it ships. The same termbase guides translation up front and validates it after. Concepts: Terminology; storage models: TM & termbase storage.

The working artifact is a glossary your team already maintains, for example a CSV:

  • Desktop
  • Agent (Claude)
  • CLI

The Termbases view is the glossary's home:

  1. Import the glossary. Import the CSV into the project termbase; terms are listed by language pair and domain, ready to browse and search.
  2. Spot-check a term. Look up an entry to confirm the approved target before a full pass.
  3. Enforce it. With the termbase in the project, term findings surface in the Checks view, in the terminology gate of check --ship, and against each unit in the Review queue — the same drift, caught wherever you look.

Ask for enforcement, or for curation:

Check the French files against our glossary and fix what drifts.

The assistant looks up approved terms before it writes (kapi terms lookup), runs term-check after, and fixes flagged targets through the round-trip. It can also grow the glossary: candidate terms it spots (or kapi exec term-extract surfaces) land as kind:"term" change-set entries through kapi apply — written to the committed glossary source, for you to approve in git diff.

Import once into a named termbase, verify, then check deliveries:

kapi terms import glossary.csv --name product-terms \
  --format csv --header -s en -t fr                 # once; persists across sessions
kapi terms lookup "log in" --name product-terms -s en -t fr
kapi exec term-check messages_fr.json \
  --source-lang en --target-lang fr --termbase product-terms

The term-check tool flags targets that don't use the approved equivalent for a source term — "code secret" where the terms store says "mot de passe" — and exits non-zero on violations, so it doubles as a CI gate. Scale with globs and -j 4; keep the terms store current with re-imports (concepts merge) and kapi terms export for vendor kits. Flags: kapi terms, kapi exec term-check.

In a project, the check resolves the project termbase and the recipe's languages automatically — no flags — and the terminology gate folds into kapi check --ship.
