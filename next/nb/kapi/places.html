Place content in markets and areas

A campaign written for teenagers in Oslo and a price list for the Nordic countries are each written for a place. kapi records that place as a point on a geo axis, and a rule kept for a wider place holds for the content at every place inside it. A rule kept for Norway reaches the Oslo campaign without a copy for Oslo.

This guide walks through the KapiMart sample, which Kapi Desktop opens with Try the sample. Run the commands in its folder.

Look a place up​

A geo axis takes place ids. kapi knows the world, the UN regions, every country and every country's subdivisions (counties, states, provinces). Countries and subdivisions go by their ISO codes in lower case. Look one up by name or id:

kapi context places oslo
oslo          Oslo          locality     world › europe › northern-europe › no › no-03 › oslo
no-03         Oslo          subdivision  world › europe › northern-europe › no › no-03
greater-oslo  Greater Oslo  metro        world › europe › northern-europe › no › greater-oslo

Each line gives the id, the name, the type and the path from the world down. no is Norway and no-03 is the county of Oslo. The other two places come from the sample's recipe. --within <id> lists the places directly below one place, and --json prints the answer as data.

Declare your own places​

A city, a neighbourhood or a sales territory is a place you declare under places: in kapi.yaml. Each place either sits within places or is made of places:

kapi.yaml
places:
  nordics:
    name: Nordics
    of: [no, se, dk, fi, is]
  oslo:
    name: Oslo
    type: locality
    within: no-03
    centroid: [59.91, 10.75]
    refs: [wikidata:Q585, geonames:3143244]
  grunerlokka:
    name: Grünerløkka
    type: neighbourhood
    within: oslo
  greater-oslo:
    name: Greater Oslo
    type: metro
    of: [no-03, no-32]

Oslo sits within the county no-03, so everything kapi knows about Norway holds there too. The Nordics are made of five countries, so a rule for the Nordics holds in each of them. Greater Oslo is made of two counties, and anything at Oslo or Grünerløkka sits inside it.

What contains what comes from these lines alone. A centroid and a shape file only tell the map where to draw a place.

type names the kind of place (locality, district, neighbourhood, metro, territory and others); a place made of places is a territory when you leave it out. refs record the place's ids in Wikidata, GeoNames and similar gazetteers. The project file reference lists every key.

Make the market a geo axis​

Name the axis under axes: and give it kind: geo. The axis then takes place ids, and kapi checks every value against them when it loads the recipe:

kapi.yaml
axes:
  market:
    kind: geo
    label: Market
  age:
    kind: range
    label: Reader age
    unit: years

Put content at a place​

A collection sits at a market the way it sits at a brand. The sample's Oslo youth campaign is written for readers aged 13 to 17 in Oslo, and its pick-up page is about one neighbourhood, so that item sits at Grünerløkka:

kapi.yaml
collections:
  - name: Oslo youth campaign
    base: campaigns/oslo
    channel: kapimart/marketing
    coordinates:
      market: oslo
      age: 13-17
    content:
      - path: "en/grunerlokka.md"
        target: "{lang}"
        coordinates:
          market: grunerlokka
      - path: "en/*.md"
        target: "{lang}"

A value that names no place stops the load and says what would work:

Error: invalid project file: collections[4]: collection "Oslo youth campaign": coordinates.market: axis market: "Oslo" is not a place. Write it in lower case: oslo

With no arguments, kapi context places lists the places your recipe declares:

kapi context places
greater-oslo  Greater Oslo  metro          world › europe › northern-europe › no › greater-oslo
grunerlokka   Grünerløkka   neighbourhood  world › europe › northern-europe › no › no-03 › oslo › grunerlokka
nordics       Nordics       territory      world › europe › northern-europe › nordics
oslo          Oslo          locality       world › europe › northern-europe › no › no-03 › oslo

Keep a rule for Norway​

In Norway the address form asks for the county, where other markets ask for a state or province. Note it from the store's strings and place it in Norway:

kapi context note --term County --instead-of "State / Province" \
  --seen-in src/en/checkout-flow.json --point market=no
#0q9cg2vjqx  2026-10-17T11:32:41Z  person  observe  term "County", not "State / Province"  [suggested]

A note holds where its evidence was seen: here, the store's strings, at the kapimart/store channel. --point market=no narrows it from the store's market: world to Norway. The Oslo campaign ships on the marketing channel, so the note reaches none of its pages yet. Keep it, and apply it past the channel it was seen on:

kapi context review --keep 0q9cg2vjqx --widen-to channel
#0q9cg9507h  2026-10-17T11:32:48Z  person  keep  of #0q9cg2vjqx
  written to the project's terms store

Your id differs; kapi context review lists the waiting suggestions with theirs.

See it reach the Oslo content​

Ask what applies to the campaign's page:

kapi context campaigns/oslo/en/summer-pass.md
Say this, not that:
...
- County, not "State / Province" [en]
...

The rule was kept for Norway, the page sits at Oslo, and Oslo sits within Norway, so the rule holds there. It holds at the Grünerløkka page as well. The store's strings sit at market: world, wider than Norway, so the rule stays out of them, and kapi says so:

kapi context src/en/checkout-flow.json
Keep as it is in this file:
- "State / Province" is correct here; do not rename it to "County". That rename holds only in campaigns/oslo/en/.

See it on the map​

Open the sample in Kapi Desktop, choose Explore in the Context hub, and open Map. The map opens at the narrowest place, a country at most, that holds every record, and drills down from there:

  1. Click Norway to see its counties. The county of Oslo is filled by the records that sit there; the others are hatched where a rule kept for Norway or the Nordics holds.
  2. Click Oslo to see the places the recipe declares inside it. Oslo is drawn as a circle at its centroid. A declared place with a shape: file is drawn by its outline, and one with neither is listed beside the map.
  3. Hover Greater Oslo beside the map to outline the two counties it is made of. Click a place to keep only the records that hold there.

Grid draws one equal tile per country instead, so a large country weighs the same as a small one. Explore your context walks through the rest of the view.
