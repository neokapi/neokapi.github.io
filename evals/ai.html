C

Context

architecture

What governs content at the point it sits: the coordinates, the voice profile, the terms, and the content memory holding what was already approved. Rests on the engine putting the right content in front of the governance.

M

Multilingual

architecture

The call itself: what goes into a prompt, how many blocks travel together, and how the text was split before any of that. Rests on the context layer having resolved what applies before a prompt is built.

A

Assurance

architecture

The checks that read finished content and report what breaks the rules, and whether they are right about it. Rests on every layer below, since a check reports on what they produced.
